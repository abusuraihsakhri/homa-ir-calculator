Method & interpretation

HOMA1 model
Insulin resistanceHOMA1-IR = (G × I) / 22.5
β-cell functionHOMA1-%B = (20 × I) / (G − 3.5)

G is fasting glucose in mmol/L; I is fasting insulin in µU/mL. Glucose in mg/dL is divided by 18 before calculation.

Important limitations

There is no universal diagnostic HOMA-IR cutoff. Results depend on assay, population and clinical context. HOMA1 is an approximation and is not equivalent to Oxford HOMA2 or a direct insulin-sensitivity measurement.

HOMA1-%B is undefined when glucose is ≤ 3.5 mmol/L. Use fasting samples collected together. Do not use the result alone to diagnose a condition or guide treatment.
